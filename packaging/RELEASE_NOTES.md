## Windows download

Download **CityScene-*-windows-x64.zip** below, unzip it anywhere you can write to (e.g. Documents), and
double-click **CityScene.exe**. No Python or other installs are needed.

- The map UI opens in your browser at http://127.0.0.1:8765/. Keep the console window open while you use it.
- On first start DSFTool is downloaded from Laminar Research (files.x-plane.com) and checked against a SHA-256.
- X-Plane 12 is found automatically; both paths can be changed under "Local settings".
- Jobs and generated scenery are stored inside the unzipped folder; "Install to X-Plane" links the pack into
  Custom Scenery (quit X-Plane first).

Requirements: Windows 10/11 x64, X-Plane 12, an internet connection.

Windows SmartScreen may warn because the executable is not code-signed: choose "More info" → "Run anyway".

Scenery you generate contains OpenStreetMap data (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0).
